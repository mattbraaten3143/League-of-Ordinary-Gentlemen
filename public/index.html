                  Trade finder

                  Starting points, not offers

                  The finder tries every one- and two-asset package from the other side whose OGSN value (built on FantasyCalc) is within 80–125% of the chosen asset, then checks each deal from both managers' point of view with the same rules as the calculator: value, change to the best starting lineup (Dynasty Daddy rest-of-season values), future value, and whether it fits a contender or a rebuild. Deals that hurt either side are left out, and so are deals that cost a rebuilding team future value or weaken a contender's lineup (the other team's lineup may dip by up to 3%). Win-win means both sides come out with a good tag; close value means within 10% of the deal's total value. Injured-reserve players are not offered.

Picks top out at Anchors, except next year's pick projected at 1.01, the only pick that can be Elite. FantasyCalc's own numbers are not reproduced here.

                  The season race

                  How it works: each week every manager gets one vote per award, for any team but their own. Voting is anonymous: only the commissioner's tally reads the ballots, and this page shows only who is leading each award. The totals update once a week, on Tuesday morning after that week's voting closes, and at the end of the season the manager (or player) with the most votes across all the weekly ballots wins the award.
